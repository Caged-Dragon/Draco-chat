Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Dragon Chat (“Dragon Chat”, “we”, “us”) is a messaging application built and operated by Caged Dragon Studios. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Dragon Chat, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. By creating an account or using Dragon Chat you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

The short version: we collect only what is needed to run a chat app — your account, your profile, the messages, files, statuses and call records you create, and basic technical data. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider — we cannot read it) and the username you choose. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your email address and basic profile name from that provider.
  • Profile details: avatar picture, status message and presence status (online, away, busy, do not disturb, offline).
  • Content you create: text messages, replies, edits, reactions, pinned messages, GIFs, voice messages, photos and files you attach, group names and pictures, and 24-hour statuses (text or image).
  • Social connections: friend requests and friendships, group memberships, and people you block.
  • Reports: if you report a user or message, the reason you provide and the related message reference.
  • Preferences: theme colours, chat wallpapers, per-chat settings such as disappearing-message timers, and mute or archive choices.

Information generated by using Dragon Chat

  • Activity data: message timestamps, read receipts, “last seen” time, typing indicators, which statuses you have viewed, and group read markers.
  • Call records: who called whom, whether the call was audio or video, whether it was completed, missed or declined, and its duration. We do not record the audio or video of calls.
  • Push notification data: if you enable notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription (an endpoint address and encryption keys) so we can deliver notifications to your device.
  • Technical data: an account identifier and numeric user number, session tokens stored in your browser, and standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and request times) kept by our infrastructure providers for security and reliability.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts or address book, your precise location, payment information, or any government identity numbers. Dragon Chat does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Who can see your information

  • Everyone signed in to Dragon Chat can find you by username and see your username, avatar, status message, presence status, last-seen time and numeric user number. Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • Your friends can see your active statuses and can message and call you. If you block someone, they can no longer message you or send you a friend request.
  • Direct messages are visible to the sender and the recipient. Group messages are visible to the members of that group. Forwarding, screenshots or copying by a recipient is outside our control.
  • Files and images you upload are stored at web addresses that are hard to guess, but anyone who has the exact link can open the file. Do not share a link publicly if you want a file to stay private.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protected at rest by our infrastructure provider, but they can be accessed by systems and authorised personnel operating the service when necessary for security, support or legal reasons. Please do not send highly sensitive information such as passwords or financial credentials.

5. Voice and video calls

Calls use WebRTC, which tries to connect your device directly to the other participant’s device. To do this, the participants’ IP addresses are exchanged through our signalling and the STUN/TURN servers listed above, which means the person you call can technically learn your IP address. Dragon Chat asks your browser for permission to use your microphone and camera; you can revoke this at any time in your browser or device settings. Audio and video are not recorded or stored by us.

6. Retention & deletion

  • Account and content are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Disappearing messages: if a chat’s disappearing-message timer is on, new messages are hidden after the chosen time and permanently deleted from our database shortly afterwards (a scheduled clean-up runs regularly). Files that were attached may remain in storage until they are removed.
  • Statuses disappear for friends after 24 hours and are removed from our database after a short grace period.
  • Deleting a message marks it as deleted for everyone in the conversation; the removal of the underlying data follows our regular clean-up cycles.
  • Deleting your account: contact us (see section 12) and we will delete your profile and the content linked to it, including messages you sent, within a reasonable period, except where we must keep limited information for legal reasons or to resolve abuse reports. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule.
  • Messages you sent remain visible in other people’s copies of a conversation unless they are deleted; we cannot erase content that others have already copied or saved.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections, row-level access controls in our database so users can only reach data they are entitled to, per-user storage folders, and restricted access to administrative systems. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong, unique password, keep your device secure and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Access and correct: you can view and edit your username, avatar and status message in the app.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on or off in the app or your browser settings.
  • Permissions: control microphone, camera and notification access in your browser or device settings.
  • Visibility: set your presence status, block users, and use disappearing messages.
  • Delete, export or withdraw consent: contact us to request a copy of your data, deletion of your account, or to withdraw consent for processing that depends on it. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the service to you.
  • Legal rights: depending on where you live — including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where applicable, the GDPR or similar laws — you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to or port your data, to nominate someone to exercise your rights, and to complain to your data-protection authority. We will respond to valid requests within the time required by applicable law.

9. Children

Dragon Chat is intended for people aged 18 and over. People under 18 may use it only with the verifiable consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without that consent, and we do not track or target children with advertising. If you believe a child has created an account without consent, contact us and we will remove it.

10. International processing

Our infrastructure providers operate data centres in several countries, including in the Asia-Pacific region, and your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards and contractual protections with those providers.

11. Changes to this policy
